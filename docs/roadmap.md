# Roadmap

Three states, kept deliberately separate. Roadmap items are never presented as implemented.

## Current

Implemented and working in the present build.

- Risk scoring with evidence factors and severity levels
- Entity-level intelligence and a decision workflow
- Pattern, early-warning and similarity detection (indicative)
- Investigation workspace with analyst notes
- Side-by-side dataset comparison
- Schema discovery, field mapping and data-quality grading
- Management summaries and evidence-only statistical intelligence
- Offline, in-browser operation

## Evolving

Working, and under active development or validation.

- Projection and what-if simulation (estimated, not yet backtested)
- Rule-based assistant for questions about the loaded data
- Understanding unfamiliar datasets (universal import)
- Reading guides and clearer statistical explanations
- Reliability of imports on large and irregular exports

## Future / exploration

Concepts being evaluated. Not built.

- Backtesting against a longitudinal archive of historical periods
- Control charts across many sequential periods
- Scheduled, repeatable data intake and comparison
- AI-assisted analyst workflows, such as drafting evidence summaries for human review

## Reading this roadmap

"Future" items are opportunities, not commitments. The AI-assisted item describes a possible way to help a person prepare material for review. It does not describe an existing capability, and it does not describe autonomous decision-making.
